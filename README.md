# Delta Air Lines Equity Research & Valuation Model

Equity research and valuation project on **Delta Air Lines (NYSE: DAL)** using SEC filings, airline operating KPIs, management guidance, forward financial forecasts, scenario analysis, and FCFE valuation.

## Project Objective

The goal of this project is to evaluate Delta Air Lines from a public-markets perspective by connecting:

- historical financial performance
- airline operating drivers
- management guidance
- forward earnings and cash-flow forecasts
- valuation
- investment risks and catalysts

The analysis is designed to replicate the structure of a simplified sell-side equity research workflow.

---

## Investment View

**Rating / View:** HOLD / NEUTRAL  
**Market Price at Model Date:** ~$74.63  
**Base-Case Fair Value:** ~$80.34/share  
**Implied Upside:** ~7.7%

### Core Investment Debate

Can Delta's strong premium demand, pricing power, loyalty ecosystem, and diversified revenue streams offset elevated fuel costs and non-fuel cost inflation?

The model indicates continued revenue strength but relatively limited valuation upside in the base case, resulting in a **HOLD / NEUTRAL** view.

## Airline Operating Drivers

The analysis incorporates key airline operating metrics including:

- **RPM** — Revenue Passenger Miles
- **ASM** — Available Seat Miles
- **Load Factor**
- **Passenger Yield**
- **PRASM** — Passenger Revenue per Available Seat Mile
- **TRASM** — Total Revenue per Available Seat Mile
- **CASM** — Cost per Available Seat Mile
- **CASM-Ex** — Non-fuel unit cost
- Fuel gallons consumed
- Average fuel price per gallon

These operating drivers are used to connect airline capacity, passenger demand, pricing, and unit costs to financial performance.

## Valuation

### Primary Method: FCFE DCF

The project uses a **Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) Discounted Cash Flow model** as the primary valuation framework.

Key base-case assumptions include:

- Cost of Equity: **10.0%**
- Terminal Growth Rate: **2.5%**
- Diluted Shares Outstanding: ~658 mm

### Base-Case Valuation

**Implied Equity Value:** ~$52.9B  
**Implied Value Per Share:** ~$80.34

Compared with the model-date market price of ~$74.63, the base case implies approximately **7.7% upside**.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Factors

- Strong premium travel demand
- Continued pricing / yield strength
- Growth in loyalty and American Express economics
- Premium revenue mix expansion
- Cargo and MRO growth
- Debt reduction and balance-sheet improvement
- Potential fuel-price normalization

### Key Risks

- Elevated fuel costs
- Non-fuel unit-cost inflation
- Airline pricing cyclicality
- Capacity competition
- Macroeconomic slowdown
- High operating leverage
- Margin sensitivity to external shocks

### Conclusion

Delta continues to demonstrate strong demand, pricing, and premium revenue momentum.

However, elevated fuel and non-fuel operating costs constrain near-term earnings and margins. With the base-case valuation only modestly above the market price, the model does not currently provide enough valuation cushion for a high-conviction BUY rating.

**Final View: HOLD / NEUTRAL**
